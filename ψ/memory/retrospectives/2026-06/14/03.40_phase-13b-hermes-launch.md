## Lessons Learned

1. **Decision velocity is meta-performance**: In high-stakes sessions (launch prep), the ability to present options + recommend + move on the user's signal is more valuable than perfecting any one task. This session shipped because Tham could say "yes" or "no" quickly.

2. **Non-blocking failures should escalate immediately to user**: When you hit a blocker and the task is not critical path, ask the user "skip or fix?" within minutes, not after cycling on failed solutions. Vitest was a good example: "skip now" was the right call, but I wasted 30 min before offering it.

3. **Comprehensive docs unlock parallel execution**: By writing detailed phase plans (Hermes 1–3), I removed ambiguity for Tham, Codex-01, Khun-Ram to work independently. Good planning is force multiplication for async teams.

4. **Automation compounds**: Simple choices (automated reminders, scheduled follow-ups) prevent manual context-switching and free mental bandwidth for the next decision. Worth the upfront 10 minutes to prevent 30 minutes of "did we send the reminder?" anxiety.
