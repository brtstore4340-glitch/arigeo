## Lessons Learned

1. **When a hook isn't in settings files after one grep pass, escalate immediately** — don't do a second search pass with slightly different flags. "Not in settings" after one comprehensive search = "it's in the platform/harness layer." Say that and move on.

2. **Zero-task sessions will always fail "all tasks pass" hooks** — any pure orientation, identity, or meta session has this property. The fix must be at the hook level (guard for empty task list), not at the session level.
