# PPTX Brief Extract

## Project intent

One corporate website to introduce ARIGEO and connect users to its household and skincare portfolio.

## Brand families

- Captainmaid — Household
- GenuLeaf — Skincare
- CeraTory — Derma skincare

## Strategic goals

1. Corporate credibility
2. Brand discovery
3. Launch-ready CMS

## UX direction

- Kao Global inspired information architecture and brand gateway behavior
- ARIGEO visual identity remains white/red/black
- Simple navigation
- Category-first discovery
- Trust-building content

## Scope

- About/ARIGEO Way
- Brands and product portfolio
- Product filters and detail pages
- Innovation
- Sustainability/responsibility
- Newsroom
- Careers
- Contact
- TH/EN
- SEO
- Enquiry/partnership/career routing forms
- CMS modules
- GA4/GTM

## Visual direction

Nature-science, bright and clean, generous whitespace, strong readability, clear CTA hierarchy and no clutter.
